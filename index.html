Interactive infographics every Dimau app can pick from

Original ways to explore unfamiliar information: networks, amounts, levels, timelines and headline-number cards. Each app loads one shared script, chooses the template that fits the shape of its information, and supplies its own data and colours.

  • ConnectionsLanternfield: topics and the links between them
  • AmountsTidepool: budgets and trade-offs
  • LevelsStrata: catalogues and categories
  • TimeRiverline, Switchback, Swimlane and Roadmap
  • StepsWaypath: journeys and processes
  • Headline numbersSignal cards with share images
  • Rankings and flowsRankrace, Flowbank, Peoplegrid
  • Tables and storiesLedger, Storyscroll, Guessline

Tidepool

Amounts

Amounts are vessels joined by pipes. Raise one and the water visibly drains from the others, so a trade-off is something you see, not a sum you do.

Timelines · one dataset, four views

Timelines

Every timeline view reads the same data, so an app can switch view without reshaping anything. Riverline's data works in all of them.

  • SwitchbackVertical story: alternating cards, reading progress, year rail, story mode.
  • SwimlaneLanes of points and spans with zoom, a navigator strip and a detail panel.
  • RoadmapGantt plan with milestones and dependencies. Drag a task and see what moves.
  • WaypathStops on a road, cycle or staircase. Tap to reveal, press play to walk it.
  • RiverlineTime as a river that widens where more happened.

Charts · the formats the best data sites use

Charts

Original versions of the formats the leading data-journalism and chart sites are known for: rankings that race, unit charts, draw-your-guess, flows, living tables and scroll-driven stories.

  • RankraceBars overtake each other as time plays; scrub to any moment.
  • PeoplegridOne square per thing; regroup and watch the squares move.
  • GuesslineDraw your guess, then see the real line and your score.
  • FlowbankFlows sized by amount; tap one to follow it.
  • LedgerSort, search, bars in cells, rows that open.
  • StoryscrollThe chart stays put while the story scrolls past.
