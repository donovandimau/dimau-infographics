    How to explore
    • The lantern magnifies and names what is under it. Inside the rim the pointer roams freely; past the rim it pulls the lantern along.
    • Rim compass: dots on the rim point to related topics outside the lantern. Click one to bring it in. Ringed dots are unexplored.
    • Fog stays where you have not looked yet.
    • Pins: double-click or press P to leave a lantern. Pin two areas to see every link between them.
    • Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, [ and ] change the lantern size.
