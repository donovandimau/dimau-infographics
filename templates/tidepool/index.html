Amounts · budgets and trade-offs

Tidepool

Each amount is a vessel joined to the next by a pipe. Drag a water level, or focus a vessel and use the arrow keys. The total stays the same, so the water you add comes out of the unlocked vessels. Lock a vessel to protect it.
