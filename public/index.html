Template library · v1

Interactive infographics every Dimau app can pick from

Four original ways to explore unfamiliar information. Each app loads one shared script, chooses the template that fits the shape of its information, and supplies its own data and colours.

  • ConnectionsLanternfield: topics and the links between them
  • AmountsTidepool: budgets and trade-offs
  • LevelsStrata: catalogues and categories
  • TimeRiverline: histories and timelines

Lanternfield

Connections

A lantern magnifies whatever it passes over, its rim points to related topics outside it, and fog marks what the reader has not looked at yet.

Tidepool

Amounts

Amounts are vessels joined by pipes. Raise one and the water visibly drains from the others, so a trade-off is something you see, not a sum you do.

Open the full demo

Strata

Levels

Each level is a core sample of layers sized by value. A strip in every layer previews what is inside; pull one out to drill down, and the depth gauge leads back up.

Open the full demo

Riverline

Time

Time flows as a river that widens wherever more happened. A magnifying current spreads out the busy years under the pointer.
