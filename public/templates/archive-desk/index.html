Archive Desk

A 3D book with turning pages beside a stack of pictures. Turn a page and its picture comes forward; tap a picture and the book turns to its page. Watch Images opens a lightbox with a magnifying loupe and a visual story that plays itself; Read Book gives a page or scroll view, text size and parchment or sepia light.
